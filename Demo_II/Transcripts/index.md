# Transcripts

Raw and lightly cleaned recordings of WorldSim design meetings.

Transcripts are machine-generated (Whisper, run offline) and then lightly cleaned. Speech is mostly Ukrainian with switches into Russian and Polish, the audio has background noise, and speakers are **not separated** — expect attribution errors. Unrecoverable passages are marked `[нерозбірливо]` / `[unintelligible]`; filler and noise fragments were dropped.

Each transcript ships both the verbatim original and an English translation. Both are preserved deliberately: the translation is a reading aid, the original is the record.
